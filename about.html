About WattCalc Project

WattCalc Project is being built as a calculator-first home-energy resource. Our goal is to make energy-cost questions easier to answer with transparent formulas, editable assumptions and links to primary sources where useful.

Editorial approach

We separate mathematical estimates from product claims. Every calculator shows the inputs that drive its result. When a device has variable power draw, we describe the result as a scenario instead of pretending one number fits every home.
