How Much Electricity Does a Space Heater Use?

An electric resistance heater converts electrical input into heat at the point of use, so its electrical rating is a useful starting point for a cost scenario. A heater labeled 1,500 W uses 1.5 kWh for each full hour it actually draws 1,500 W.

Full-power runtimeEnergy at 1,500 W
1 hour1.5 kWh
4 hours6 kWh
8 hours12 kWh

Thermostatic cycling can reduce actual runtime, so the full-power table is a scenario rather than a guarantee.
