How Much Electricity Does a Space Heater Use?

Electric resistance heaters are relatively straightforward when the heating element is active: energy use is electrical power multiplied by time. A heater labeled 1,500 W uses 1.5 kWh for each full hour it actually draws 1,500 W.

Why the thermostat matters

Once the room approaches the thermostat setting, the element may cycle off. Four hours of “heater on” time therefore does not always mean four full-power hours.

Cost example

At the June 2026 U.S. residential average of 18.34¢/kWh, one full-power hour at 1.5 kW is about $0.28. Your state or utility rate may be very different.

Best input

Use the wattage on the label and adjust active runtime if the heater cycles. For the most accurate personal result, use measured kWh.
