How to Estimate Electricity Cost From Watts and Hours

Most household electricity-cost estimates can be reduced to two steps: estimate energy use in kilowatt-hours, then multiply that energy by your utility rate.

Step 1: convert watts to kilowatts

Divide watts by 1,000. A 1,500-watt heater is 1.5 kW.

Step 2: multiply by runtime

If that heater draws 1.5 kW for 4 hours, the simple energy estimate is 6 kWh.

Step 3: multiply by your rate

At $0.18/kWh, 6 kWh would cost $1.08. Use your own effective utility rate because tariffs vary.

Where this gets tricky

Many appliances cycle on and off or change power dynamically. A nameplate maximum is therefore not always the same as average power. For those devices, measured energy consumption produces a better estimate.
