How to Estimate Electricity Cost From Watts and Hours

Most appliance cost estimates start with energy, not dollars. Convert watts to kilowatts, multiply by time, then multiply the resulting kWh by your electricity price.

Core formula

kWh = watts ÷ 1,000 × hours. A 1,000 W device at full power for 5 hours uses 5 kWh. At 18.34¢/kWh, that simple scenario costs about $0.92.

Add duty cycle when the load cycles

Compressors, thermostats and inverter equipment may not draw their entered wattage for every scheduled hour. If the device averages full power for roughly 60% of an eight-hour window, multiply by 0.60.

Use the right electricity rate

WattCalc provides June 2026 EIA state averages as benchmarks, but your own bill's effective $/kWh rate is a better personal input.

Rate source: EIA Electric Power Monthly Table 5.6.A.
