Electricity cost guide

Ceiling Fan Electricity Cost

Ceiling fans are relatively steady electrical loads compared with compressors and thermostatic heaters, so watts × hours is usually a useful first estimate.

Use the right wattage

Fan power can change by speed. Use the input watts for the speed you normally use or measure the fan if practical. If a light kit runs at the same time, add its wattage separately.

Formula

Daily kWh = watts ÷ 1,000 × hours per day. Multiply by your $/kWh rate for daily cost, then scale to month or year.
