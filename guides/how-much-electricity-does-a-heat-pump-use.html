How Much Electricity Does a Heat Pump Use?

There is no single kWh figure for every heat pump. Electrical use changes with system size, outdoor temperature, thermostat setting, building load, defrost behavior and compressor speed.

Heating capacity is not electrical power

A BTU/h heating-capacity rating tells you useful heating output, not directly how many electrical watts the system is drawing. For cost estimates, use electrical input or measured kWh.

Why a range is useful

Variable-speed systems can modulate. Run a low, middle and high active-runtime case to see how sensitive the bill estimate is to weather and load.

The U.S. Department of Energy explains that heat pumps transfer heat rather than generate it directly, and discusses staged and variable-speed systems. DOE heat pump systems.
