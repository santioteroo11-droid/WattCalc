How Much Electricity Does a Heat Pump Use?

There is no single kWh number that applies to every heat pump. Electrical draw changes with equipment size, outdoor temperature, thermostat set point, building heat loss, defrost operation and compressor speed.

The U.S. Department of Energy explains that heat pumps transfer heat rather than generate it directly, which is why they can provide efficient heating and cooling. For cost planning, use measured or manufacturer electrical input plus a realistic runtime assumption.

Avoid a common mistake

Heating capacity in BTU/h is not the same thing as electrical input in watts. Do not divide or multiply capacity by your electricity price without converting through the equipment's actual electrical performance.
