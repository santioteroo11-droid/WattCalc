How Much Does a Dehumidifier Cost to Run?

A dehumidifier can be plugged in for many hours without drawing compressor power continuously. The humidistat cycles the compressor as room humidity changes, which makes duty cycle important.

Two useful estimation methods

If you know average operating watts, multiply kW by active hours. If product documentation or an energy monitor gives annual or daily kWh, use the measured energy directly and multiply by your electricity rate.

What changes consumption?

  • Target humidity and moisture load
  • Room or basement temperature
  • Capacity and efficiency
  • Fan and compressor cycling
  • Air leakage and drainage setup
