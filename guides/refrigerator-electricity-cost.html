Refrigerator Electricity Cost

A refrigerator is powered all day, but its compressor does not normally draw full running power for all 24 hours. That is why annual kWh is a better cost input than simply multiplying nameplate watts by 24.

Where to find the number

Look for annual energy use on the EnergyGuide label, manufacturer specifications or an ENERGY STAR model listing. Multiply that kWh/year value by your electricity rate.

Example method

If a label says 365 kWh/year and your rate is $0.18/kWh, the arithmetic is 365 × $0.18. Replace both numbers with your own model and tariff.
