Electricity cost guide

Window Air Conditioner Electricity Cost

Window AC electricity cost depends on electrical input and how long the compressor needs to run. Cooling capacity in BTU/h is not itself a wattage.

Efficiency context

CEER is an efficiency metric used for room air conditioners. For two units delivering similar cooling, the more efficient unit generally requires less electrical energy under standardized conditions.

Best input for the calculator

Use model-specific input watts or measured kWh. Then adjust runtime or duty cycle for your room and climate.
