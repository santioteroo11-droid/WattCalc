Portable AC Electricity Cost

A portable AC's BTU rating describes cooling capacity, not electrical input. For cost calculations, use input watts or measured kWh.

Why runtime is uncertain

Room heat gain, outdoor temperature, thermostat setting, hose configuration and insulation all change compressor runtime. A low/mid/high duty-cycle range is more informative than assuming full power all day.

Simple formula

Cost = input watts ÷ 1,000 × active hours × $/kWh.
