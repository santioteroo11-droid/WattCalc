Residential electricity rates by state — June 2026

WattCalc uses the U.S. Energy Information Administration's June 2026 residential average electricity prices as optional calculator presets. The U.S. average was 18.34¢/kWh.

These are statewide residential averages, not your exact utility tariff. For personal budgeting, the all-in rate on your electricity bill is usually the better input.

How WattCalc uses this data

Every state-rate selector fills the calculator's editable $/kWh field. You can overwrite it at any time with your own rate, so the state table is a benchmark rather than a hidden assumption.
