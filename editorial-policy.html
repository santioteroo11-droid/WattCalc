Editorial & Research Policy

WattCalc prioritizes reproducible calculations and primary sources.

Source hierarchy

  1. Government and standards sources such as EIA, DOE and ENERGY STAR.
  2. Manufacturer or model-specific documentation.
  3. Measured energy data when it applies to the user's own equipment.
  4. Illustrative examples clearly labeled as examples.

AI-assisted work

AI may assist with research organization, drafting and code, but a page should not be published simply because it can be generated. Calculator formulas, source claims, navigation and user value are reviewed before publication.

Corrections and updates

Corrections can be submitted through the public repository issue tracker. Benchmark data is updated when new source releases materially improve the calculators.

Advertising and affiliate content

If monetization relationships are introduced, relevant disclosures will be added and will not change the arithmetic of calculator results.
