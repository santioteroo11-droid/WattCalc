Editorial & Research Policy

Our calculator pages use explicit formulas and user-supplied inputs whenever practical. We favor primary sources such as U.S. Department of Energy documentation for technical background.

AI use

AI tools may assist with drafting, code and research organization, but pages should not be published solely because they can be generated. Each public page must be checked for usefulness, calculations, navigation and factual accuracy.

Updates

Pages should be revised when source guidance changes or Search Console data reveals a user question the page does not yet answer.
