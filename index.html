Why results are estimates

Real electricity use depends on equipment, climate, thermostat settings, cycling, tariffs and behavior. A calculator is most useful when you replace example inputs with measured energy or model-specific specifications.

Sources we prioritize

Benchmark electricity prices come from the U.S. Energy Information Administration. Technical context is checked against sources such as the U.S. Department of Energy and ENERGY STAR where applicable.
