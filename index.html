Our method

We prefer calculations you can inspect over unexplained averages. Most tools begin with the basic relationship kWh = kW × hours, then multiply energy use by the electricity rate you enter. For variable-speed equipment, results are scenarios rather than promises because actual power draw changes with load, climate, settings and equipment efficiency.
