Useful estimates without fake precision

Real electricity use changes with equipment, climate, cycling, tariffs and behavior. WattCalc keeps those assumptions visible so you can replace examples with model-specific or measured data.

Benchmark electricity prices come from the U.S. Energy Information Administration. Technical context is checked against sources such as the U.S. Department of Energy and ENERGY STAR where applicable.
