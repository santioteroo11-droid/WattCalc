Refrigerator Electricity Cost Calculator

For a refrigerator, the annual-kWh number on an EnergyGuide label or product specification is usually more useful than nameplate watts because the compressor cycles.

Quick answer: A refrigerator rated at 365 kWh/year costs about $5.58 per month or $66.94 per year at the June 2026 U.S. residential average of 18.34¢/kWh.
Use the EnergyGuide label, manufacturer data or measured annual kWh.

Why annual kWh is better here

For appliances that cycle automatically, annual energy use usually describes real consumption better than multiplying nameplate watts by 24 hours.

Electricity-price presets: EIA Table 5.6.A, June 2026.

ENERGY STAR's refrigerator product listings publish model-specific annual energy use. The 365 kWh default here is only an editable example; use your own model's label for a meaningful estimate.

Where to find annual kWh

Look for the EnergyGuide label, manufacturer specifications, or a model listing. ENERGY STAR's refrigerator database includes annual energy use for individual models, so two similarly sized refrigerators can still differ. ENERGY STAR refrigerator listings.
