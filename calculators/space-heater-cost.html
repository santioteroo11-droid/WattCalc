Space Heater Electricity Cost Calculator

See what a portable electric space heater can add to your power bill. The default 1,500 W is an editable example, not a claim about every heater.

Quick answer: At the June 2026 U.S. residential average of 18.34¢/kWh, a 1,500 W space heater costs about $0.28 per full-power hour. Four full-power hours a day is about $33.01 per 30-day month; eight hours is about $66.02. Thermostat cycling can reduce actual use.

These examples assume the heating element draws 1,500 W continuously during the listed hours. A thermostat may cycle it off.

How the estimate works

kWh = watts ÷ 1,000 × hours × active-runtime percentage. Cost is then kWh multiplied by the electricity rate. The state presets use June 2026 residential averages from the U.S. Energy Information Administration.

Rate source: EIA Electric Power Monthly, Table 5.6.A. State averages are benchmarks, not utility-specific tariffs.

Many resistance heaters draw close to their rated power while actively heating, but a thermostat can cycle the heating element off. Lower the active-runtime percentage if you have a realistic cycling estimate.

Make the estimate more accurate

  • Use measured watts or measured kWh when the appliance cycles or changes speed.
  • Use the electricity rate from your own bill if available.
  • Model a low and high runtime scenario instead of relying on one number.
