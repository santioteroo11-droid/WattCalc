Window Air Conditioner Cost Calculator

Estimate what a window room air conditioner costs to run and compare state-rate scenarios.

Quick answer: A 900 W window AC running at full input for eight hours a day would cost about $39.61 per 30-day month at 18.34¢/kWh. Real compressor runtime is often lower, which is why the calculator includes duty cycle.

How the estimate works

kWh = watts ÷ 1,000 × hours × active-runtime percentage. Cost is then kWh multiplied by the electricity rate. The state presets use June 2026 residential averages from the U.S. Energy Information Administration.

Rate source: EIA Electric Power Monthly, Table 5.6.A. State averages are benchmarks, not utility-specific tariffs.

ENERGY STAR evaluates room air conditioners using efficiency metrics such as CEER. Higher efficiency can lower electrical input for the same cooling output. Use model-specific input watts or measured kWh for the tightest estimate.

Efficiency matters

ENERGY STAR publishes current room-air-conditioner efficiency criteria by capacity. Review ENERGY STAR room AC criteria.

Make the estimate more accurate

  • Use measured watts or measured kWh when the appliance cycles or changes speed.
  • Use the electricity rate from your own bill if available.
  • Model a low and high runtime scenario instead of relying on one number.
