What the comparison includes

Both scenarios use the same electricity price. Each annual cost is based on watts ÷ 1,000 × hours/day × active-runtime percentage × 365 × $/kWh.

This compares energy cost only. It does not include purchase price, repairs, maintenance, financing, rebates or different useful lifetimes.
