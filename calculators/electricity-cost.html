Electricity Cost Calculator

Estimate electricity cost from watts, hours of use, days and your electricity rate.

Formula

Energy (kWh) = watts ÷ 1,000 × hours/day × days.
Cost = energy × electricity rate.

For variable-speed equipment such as heat pumps and mini-splits, nameplate wattage may not equal average real-world draw. If possible, use measured average power or a utility/energy-monitor reading for a more realistic scenario.

What changes the result?

Your utility rate, runtime and actual electrical draw dominate the estimate. Weather, thermostat settings, insulation and equipment controls can change runtime substantially.
