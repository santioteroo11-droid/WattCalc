# WattCalc

Free, transparent home electricity-cost calculators.

Public site: https://santioteroo11-droid.github.io/WattCalc/

## Current scope

- 10 appliance / HVAC electricity-cost calculators
- 10 supporting guides
- June 2026 EIA residential electricity-rate presets for all U.S. states + D.C.
- Transparent formulas and editable assumptions
- Calculator-specific handling for duty cycle, annual kWh, and kWh per load
- Search Console verification, sitemap, canonical URLs, and responsive design

## Data sources

WattCalc prioritizes primary sources such as the U.S. Energy Information Administration (EIA), U.S. Department of Energy (DOE), and ENERGY STAR.

Educational estimates only. Real utility costs vary by tariff, equipment, climate, installation, and usage.
